t04 — The hosted service serves the same files the release ships

The bootloaders chain to boot.netboot.xyz/<version>/.
The lab fetched the live menu and compared it with the copy
inside menus.tar.gz:

$ curl -s https://boot.netboot.xyz/3.0.3/menu.ipxe -o live-menu.ipxe
$ diff live-menu.ipxe menus/menu.ipxe && echo IDENTICAL
IDENTICAL

$ curl -s https://boot.netboot.xyz/version.ipxe
#!ipxe
set upstream_version 3.x
exit

Two observations:

- The versioned path (boot.netboot.xyz/3.0.3/) serves exactly
  the release's menu files, byte for byte. Self-hosting the
  tarball is therefore not an approximation of the service;
  it is the same content, served by you.
- The unversioned version.ipxe answers "3.x" — it exists so
  an older bootloader can learn that a newer major line
  exists, which is why menu.ipxe fetches it with a short
  timeout and carries on regardless of the answer.
